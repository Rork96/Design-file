# Instagram kit — @paulrork

Recreations of the source artwork:
- **QuestionPost** — "Instagram post - 4": electric-blue 1080² post, glow-highlighted title, bulleted big question in lime, grey photo slots, handle bottom-left.
- **ListPost** — "Instagram post - 6": lime 1080² post, violet title, three bulleted statements, handle mid-right.
- **StoryTemplate** — Figma "Instagram story - 13": indigo 1080×1920 with the story header band and camera-thirds guides.
- **ColourBoard** — Figma frame "Story ideal Lines" (2226×2639): framed swatches, three colour-role labels and the guided story.

Post coordinates were measured from exported PNGs (scaled ×2.53 to 1080) and are approximate; story/board values are exact from Figma.
`index.html` lets you switch templates and toggle photo slots / guides.
